# Short-Form Video Concept

**Brand:** BrightSteps Learning (fictional)  
**Format:** 15-second Instagram Reel  
**Objective:** Encourage parents to ask about a trial English lesson  
**Audience:** Parents of children who are beginning to learn English

## Storyboard

| Time | Visual | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 sec | Parent and child look at a picture book together. | English practice can fit into everyday moments. |
| 3–6 sec | They read a menu or label together. | Read one thing together. |
| 6–10 sec | The child describes an illustration. | Take turns describing what you see. |
| 10–13 sec | Parent and child add a sentence to a story. | Make up a story together. |
| 13–15 sec | BrightSteps Learning name and simple closing screen. | Want to learn more? Message us about a trial lesson. |

## Caption

Small moments can make English practice feel more natural. Read, describe, and tell a story together—one everyday moment at a time.

Interested in learning more? Message BrightSteps Learning to ask about a trial lesson.

## Creative Notes

- Use warm, friendly visuals and clear subtitles.
- Keep each scene simple and easy to follow.
- Use gentle background music that does not compete with the words.
- This is a fictional portfolio concept. No real campaign or performance data is claimed.
