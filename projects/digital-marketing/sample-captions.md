These sample posts were created for a fictional campaign. Lesson details and the free trial offer are illustrative and would need confirmation before real publication.

## Post 1: Everyday English Carousel

**Format:** Instagram carousel  
**Purpose:** Awareness and saves

**Slide outline**
1. English practice can happen in everyday moments.
2. Read a short menu together.
3. Find objects around the home and describe their colours.
4. Take turns adding a sentence to a story.
5. Keep it playful, encouraging, and pressure-free.

**Caption**

Small moments can make room for English practice. Try reading a few words together, describing something you see, or making up a story one sentence at a time.

Which idea would your family try first?

**CTA:** Save this post for later.  
**Visual note:** Use simple illustrations and large, readable text.

## Post 2: Three Playful Practice Ideas

**Format:** Short Instagram Reel  
**Purpose:** Engagement and shares

**Storyboard**
- **Opening:** “Need a fresh idea for English practice?”
- **Scene 1:** Pick an object and describe it with three words.
- **Scene 2:** Find five items that begin with the same letter.
- **Scene 3:** Start a story with one sentence and take turns adding to it.
- **Closing:** “Keep it short, relaxed, and fun.”

**Caption**

A little practice can fit into an ordinary day. Try one of these quick activities and let your child choose the next one.

**CTA:** Share this Reel with a parent who might like these ideas.  
**Visual note:** Add captions on screen so the video is understandable without sound.
