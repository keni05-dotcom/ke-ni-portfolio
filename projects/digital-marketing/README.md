# BrightSteps Learning Trial Class Campaign

## Project context
This is a simulated portfolio project for a fictional Malaysian children’s learning centre. It applies digital marketing concepts from my Google Digital Marketing & E-commerce Professional Certificate. No real campaign has been launched, and no performance results are claimed.

## Campaign objective
Plan a two-week social media campaign to encourage parents and guardians of children aged 6–12 to enquire about a free introductory English lesson through WhatsApp.

## Intended audience
Parents and guardians in Malaysia who are looking for engaging English learning support for their children. This audience description is a planning assumption, not the result of completed market research.

## Campaign offer and message
**Offer:** A free introductory lesson (fictional campaign offer).

**Key message:** Help your child build confidence in English through engaging learning.

**Call to action:** Message BrightSteps Learning on WhatsApp to ask about a trial lesson.

## Channels
- Instagram and Facebook for campaign content
- WhatsApp for enquiries and trial lesson follow-up

## Measurement plan
- **Primary measure:** Number of trial lesson bookings attributed to the campaign
- **Supporting measures:** Reach, engagement, link clicks, WhatsApp enquiries, and enquiry-to-booking conversion
- No actual campaign data is available; results will not be presented as real.

## Planned campaign materials
- Two-week content calendar
- Sample social media posts and captions
- Short-form video concept
- Simple KPI tracking sheet
